Try to trick the agent.

Each scenario sends a payment request from an AI agent to your UPI app. Rekha checks it against 19 rules and answers allow, ask or block. The phone shows what the user sees; the trace shows why.

Decisions are computed live in this page from the same rule table as the PRD (R00–R18). The plain-language builder is a small rule-based parser standing in for the production LLM: it understands fewer phrasings, and when it is unsure it picks the stricter reading and says so. Shops, agents and UPI IDs are fictional.
